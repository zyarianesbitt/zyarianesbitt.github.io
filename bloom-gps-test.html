Bloom GPS Campus Test

Checks whether phone GPS is accurate enough for Bloom to tell campus buildings apart. Your readings stay on this phone until you send them.

How to do it

  1. Turn on location for your browser. On iPhone, also turn on Precise Location.
  2. Walk inside a building, near the middle.
  3. Pick the building below and tap Record reading.
  4. Take 3 readings per building, about 30 seconds apart.
  5. Do 4 or 5 buildings, then tap Share results or Copy results and send them to ZyAria.
